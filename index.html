A bit about me

I'm a Backend Developer based in Lagos, Nigeria with 2+ years of experience building production-grade APIs and automation systems. I specialize in the Node.js ecosystem — particularly around queue-based architectures, caching strategies, and workflow automation.

I care about writing clean, maintainable code and building systems that scale. I refactor instinctively, think about performance early, and always ask "what happens when this breaks?"
