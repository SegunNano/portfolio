Personalized News Digest Platform
GitHub ↗ Live ↗

A production-grade news aggregation and digest delivery system. Fetches 130+ articles daily across 13 categories, serves them via a category-level Redis cache shared across all users, and delivers personalized email digests on user-defined schedules via automated N8n workflows.

→ Queue-based architecture with BullMQ — async email delivery, news fetching and inactivity detection across multiple background workers
