About

Who I am.

I'm a Backend Developer & Automation Engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria with 2+ years of experience building production-grade APIs and event-driven systems. I specialize in the Node.js ecosystem — particularly around queue-based architectures, Redis caching strategies, and workflow automation.

I care deeply about writing clean, maintainable code and building systems that scale. I refactor instinctively, think about performance early, and always ask "what happens when this breaks?" before shipping.

My path into engineering is unconventional — I hold a BSc in Botany from the University of Lagos, and I'm currently pursuing a funded Master's degree in Europe focused on algae biotechnology. The scientific mindset — hypothesis, experiment, iterate — transfers directly into how I architect systems.

Currently expanding into AI integration and LLM-powered backends — OpenAI API, Ollama, and LangChain.js automation pipelines. I believe the next generation of backend engineers will be the ones who know how to wire AI into production systems reliably.
